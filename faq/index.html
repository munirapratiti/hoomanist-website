Still wondering something? Just ask us →

Do you only do recruitment?

No. Recruitment is where many teams start with us, but we also build people growth, performance and culture systems — often for the same team as they scale.

How long does a hire take?

Usually 4–8 weeks per role, from the first brief to a signed offer — with you involved at the moments that matter, not buried in CVs.

What if the hire doesn't work out?

If the candidate leaves, or both sides agree the fit isn't working within 90 days, we run a new search at no extra charge. It's built into every engagement, not a special add-on.

When do you invoice?

Upon written confirmation the candidate has accepted the offer — not at placement. If they don't start, there's no fee.

Do you work with small teams?

Yes — most of our work is with growing studios and lean in-house teams, from 5 people up. That's exactly where every people decision counts the most.

Can we hire for multiple roles at once?

Yes — each role is its own engagement, and multi-role bundles (three or more roles at once) qualify for a bundled rate.

Are you remote or on-site?

We work remotely with teams anywhere, and on-site when it helps. Wherever your team is, we plug into how you already work.
