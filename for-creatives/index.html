Roles we place

Graphic and UI designers, copywriters and content writers, social media specialists, videographers, video editors and motion designers, plus art directors and creative leads — mostly for studios, agencies and in-house brand teams.

What we look for

Craft you can show, and the way you work with people. We read portfolios properly, and we ask how you like to be briefed, managed and grown — because that is usually what decides whether a role lasts.

Before you send

Will you share my portfolio without telling me?

No. We ask you first, every time, before your name or work goes to a company.

I did not hear back. Does that mean no?

It usually means there is no matching role open right now. We keep portfolios on file and reach out when something genuinely fits, which can be months later.

I am still employed. Is that a problem?

Not at all, and we keep it confidential. Most people we place are working when they first talk to us.
